## How the pipeline works

```mermaid
flowchart LR
  n0["products"]
  n1["observations"]
  n2["inputs"]
  n3["discover"]
  n4["qualify"]
  n5["mappings"]
  n2 --> n3
  n3 --> n4
  n4 --> n5
  n0 --> n2
  n1 --> n2
  n0 --> n2
  n1 --> n3
```

- **products**: All 150 original product\-country identities, retaining original roster evidence\.
- **observations**: Retained discovery observations for rechecking matching without recollecting source pages\.
- **inputs**: Validate all product\-country identities and reuse fresh mappings until their monthly refresh\.
- **discover**: Try identifier and fallback searches, reject incompatible variants and save one high\-confidence catalog mapping with review evidence\.
- **qualify**: Recheck retained matching evidence against explicit colour, size, edition and packaging requirements before saving a catalog match\.
- **mappings**: Persistent product\-country catalog mappings, candidate evidence and acquisition failures\.

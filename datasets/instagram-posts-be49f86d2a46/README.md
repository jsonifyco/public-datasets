## How the pipeline works

```mermaid
flowchart LR
  n0["profiles"]
  n1["profile#95;posts"]
  n2["post#95;details"]
  n3["posts"]
  n0 --> n1
  n1 --> n2
  n2 --> n3
```

- **profiles**: Validate and deduplicate the supplied Instagram profile URLs, then normalize them to English pages\.
- **profile\_posts**: Read each English Instagram profile grid and pair its posts with their own preview images\.
- **post\_details**: Read each English post page for its caption, timestamp, counts and main image\.
- **posts**: Public Instagram posts with captions, preview images and English source links\.

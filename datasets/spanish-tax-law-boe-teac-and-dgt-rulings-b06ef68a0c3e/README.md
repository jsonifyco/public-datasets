## How the pipeline works

```mermaid
flowchart LR
  n0["plan"]
  n1["index"]
  n2["select"]
  n3["details"]
  n4["validate"]
  n5["main"]
  n0 --> n1
  n1 --> n2
  n2 --> n3
  n3 --> n4
  n4 --> n5
```

- **plan**: Plan official BOE daily indexes and TEAC monthly searches for the fixed sample windows\.
- **index**: Read BOE publication indexes and all pages of each bounded TEAC monthly search\.
- **select**: Deduplicate BOE dispositions and select the newest distinct TEAC resolutions with all associated criteria\.
- **details**: Retrieve each official disposition or resolution and extract its subject, summary and cited legislation\.
- **validate**: Check unique references, date windows, required fields and complete selected\-source coverage before publication\.
- **main**: Official BOE dispositions and distinct TEAC resolutions in the Spanish tax law sample\.
